Library Services Guide (Revision: 2026-01-10)

The main library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. On Saturdays it is open from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During examination week, hours may be extended; announcements are posted at the library entrance and on the student portal.

Undergraduate students may borrow up to five books for 21 days. Renewals are allowed once if no other student has placed a hold. A student ID is required to borrow materials.

These details are fictional and created only for software testing; they are not official college policy.
